Privacy Policy.

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Trace app for Android

In short: Trace keeps everything on your phone.
  • No account, no sign-up, no bank login.
  • Trace collects no personal data and has no access to the internet.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking inside the app.

What Trace stores

Trace saves the information you enter: the amount, category, note and date of each expense, your categories and budgets, and settings such as your name (optional) and currency. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nobody else, including the developer, can see it.

What Trace does not do

  • Trace does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so the app cannot send any data off your phone.
  • Trace does not read your SMS, bank or payment app notifications, contacts, location, photos or files.
  • Trace does not record or store audio. The microphone is used only while you add an expense by voice.
  • Trace does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.
  • Trace does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone, because it never collects any.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the optional lock-screen shortcut for adding an expense. You can turn it off in Trace's Settings or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup: so the lock-screen shortcut comes back after your phone restarts.
  • Microphone (optional): only if you tap the mic to add an expense by voice. Trace listens only while the "Listening…" box is open.

Adding by voice

On phones that support it, you can say an expense instead of typing it. Your speech is turned into text by Android's on-device speech recogniser, on the phone itself; it is not sent to any online service, and Trace still has no internet permission. Audio is never recorded or saved: Trace keeps only the amount, category and note you then choose to save. Phones that can't recognise speech offline don't show the mic at all.

Links that open your browser

Settings has optional links to the developer's Ko-fi page and to Trace's source code on GitHub. Tapping one opens your web browser; those sites have their own privacy policies. Trace itself sends nothing.

Backups and deleting your data

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include Trace's data in your device backup, under Google's own backup terms. You can delete any expense inside the app, and uninstalling Trace (or clearing its storage in Android settings) removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Trace is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

This website

This policy is about the app. The Trace website counts page visits and download clicks with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies and does not collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
